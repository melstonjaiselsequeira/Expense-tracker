Privacy Policy & Data Notice

Transparency, security, and user data rights are fundamental to ExpenseFlow. This notice details how we collect, process, safeguard, and delete your personal and financial data.

1. Identity of the Data Fiduciary

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India, ExpenseFlow acts as the Data Fiduciary with respect to the personal and transactional data you provide when using our expense management web platform and related services.

Data Fiduciary Contact:
ExpenseFlow Technologies
Email: privacy@expenseflow.com | grievance@expenseflow.com
Jurisdiction: Republic of India

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

ExpenseFlow adheres to the principle of data minimization. We collect strictly the information necessary to provide personal budgeting, financial transaction tracking, and secure account management:

Category Specific Data Points Purpose & Necessity
Identity & Account Full name, username, email address. Account registration, email verification, multi-device identification, and service notifications.
Security Credentials Password (salted PBKDF2:SHA256 hash only). Secure authentication. Plaintext passwords are never stored, logged, or visible to system administrators.
Financial & Expense Data Expense amounts, transaction dates, spending categories (e.g., Food, Transport, Utilities), custom descriptions, and payment status. Core budgeting service: aggregate spending analytics, category breakdowns, monthly budget pacing, and statement generation.
User Uploads Profile avatars and support ticket image attachments (PNG, JPG, WEBP). Personalizing dashboard profile and troubleshooting technical support requests. Strictly optional.
Technical & Security Logs IP addresses, browser user-agent, session timestamps. Session security, automated rate limiting to prevent brute-force attacks, and audit logging.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

In accordance with Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023, your personal data is processed solely for lawful and specified purposes based on your explicit consent:

  • Service Delivery: Storing, calculating, and presenting your personal transaction ledgers and financial budget targets.
  • Export & Reporting: Generating customized on-demand CSV spreadsheets and PDF statements upon your direct request.
  • Transactional Communications: Dispatching essential security notices, email confirmation tokens, and password reset instructions.
  • Optional Monthly Reports: Delivering monthly PDF financial summaries via email only if you opt in (with the ability to opt out at any time in Account Settings).
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Enforcing rate limits, preventing session hijacking, and securing server infrastructure.

4. Data Security & Technical Safeguards

We deploy rigorous technical and organizational measures to ensure your data remains confidential and protected against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Password Protection: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and unique cryptographic salts.
  • Tenant Data Isolation: Every database query is strictly partitioned and scoped by authenticated user identity. No user can view, edit, or delete another user's financial records.
  • Session & Token Security: Isolated token salts prevent token reuse. Sessions use HTTP-only, SameSite cookies with CSRF defense.
  • File Upload Security: Uploaded attachments are scanned for permitted extensions, size-limited, renamed to prevent path traversal, and served only through authenticated access controls.
  • Rate Limiting: Automated endpoint rate limiting thwarts credential stuffing and unauthorized automated API access.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

ExpenseFlow does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or financial data to advertisers, brokers, or data analytics intermediaries.

We engage only vetted service providers ("Data Processors") strictly required for technical operations:

  • Email Delivery (Brevo / Secure SMTP Relay): Transmits automated account confirmation emails, password reset links, and requested reports. Processors operate under strict confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Database & Cloud Infrastructure: Secure relational database hosting with role-based access restrictions and encrypted data at rest and in transit.

6. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure

We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

User-Initiated Immediate Deletion:
You may permanently delete your account at any time via Dashboard > Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account.

Upon confirmation of account deletion, ExpenseFlow triggers an immediate cascading erasure process:

  • Your user account record and authentication credentials are permanently deleted from the database.
  • All associated expense transactions, categories, and monthly budget settings are permanently removed.
  • All report download history metadata is purged.
  • All support tickets and messages are deleted.
  • All uploaded profile avatar images and support ticket attachment files are permanently unlinked and erased from the server storage filesystem.

7. Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal under India's DPDP Act, 2023, you are entitled to the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access Information: You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data held by ExpenseFlow and view all processing activities directly in your Dashboard.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You can update your display name, username, or password at any time via Account Settings, and edit or correct any expense entry in real time.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You have the unconditional right to erase your personal data by deleting your account or requesting erasure from our Grievance Officer.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to readily available grievance redressal through our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an individual who, in the event of your death or incapacity, may exercise your data rights in accordance with DPDP rules.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for optional data processing (such as monthly digest emails) at any time through your preference toggles in Settings.

8. Grievance Redressal & Privacy Officer

In compliance with the DPDP Act, 2023, ExpenseFlow has designated a Grievance Officer to address any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding your personal data and privacy rights:

Grievance & Data Protection Officer:
ExpenseFlow Privacy & Compliance Team
Email: grievance@expenseflow.com
Support Portal: Contact & Support Form
Response Time: We acknowledge and address all privacy inquiries and grievance requests within the statutory timeframe prescribed under applicable DPDP regulations.

If you are not satisfied with the resolution of your grievance, you have the right to file a complaint before the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with the DPDP Act, 2023.
