Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an ExpenseFlow account, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password hash. When you use the application, you voluntarily enter financial transaction records including dates, amounts, categories, and descriptions.

2. How Your Data Is Protected

We believe financial records belong solely to their owner. ExpenseFlow implements strict user-level data isolation. Passwords are securely hashed with PBKDF2 before storage. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your transaction data to advertisers or third parties.

3. Email Communication

Your email is used strictly for authentication, account confirmation tokens, password reset links, and scheduled monthly PDF expense summaries if requested.

4. Data Ownership & Deletion

You maintain full ownership of all data entered into ExpenseFlow. You may edit, update, delete individual transaction records, or export your complete financial ledger at any time via CSV or PDF.
